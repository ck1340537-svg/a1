Editorial Policy

How we develop, test and correct recipes on Bruschetta Dawn.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Readers rely on our recipes to work in their own kitchens. These are the standards we follow when creating and maintaining content on Bruschetta Dawn.

Recipe development

Each recipe is cooked at least twice in an ordinary home kitchen with standard equipment. We adjust quantities, timings and instructions until the result is reliable, and we note common pitfalls and useful substitutions.

Writing standards

  • Ingredients are listed in the order they are used, with clear quantities.
  • Methods describe what to look for, not just how long to cook.
  • We say when a dish is inspired by, rather than faithful to, a regional tradition.
  • We avoid health and diet claims and focus on flavour and technique.

Sources

Techniques and food-safety guidance are based on established culinary knowledge and public guidance from food-safety authorities.

Independence

We do not accept payment for recipes or reviews. If advertising appears on the website, advertisers have no influence over what we publish.

Corrections

If something doesn’t work or you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed issues promptly and update the page date for meaningful changes.
