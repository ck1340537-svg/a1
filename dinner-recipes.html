Bruschetta three ways

  • Serves 4
  • 25 min
  • Easy

One loaf, three toppings: a fun, sharing-style dinner with a salad on the side.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf crusty bread, sliced and toasted
  • Classic: 3 ripe tomatoes, basil, garlic and olive oil
  • White bean: 1 can cannellini beans mashed with lemon, rosemary and olive oil
  • Mushroom: 8 oz (225 g) mushrooms sautéed with garlic and parsley

Method

  1. Prepare each topping in its own bowl and season well.
  2. Toast the bread and rub with a cut garlic clove while hot.
  3. Set everything out on a board and let everyone build their own.
  4. Serve with a big green salad dressed with lemon and olive oil.

Tip: the white bean topping keeps for two days in the fridge and makes a great lunch spread.

Hearty minestrone

  • Serves 6
  • 1 hr
  • Freezes well

A thick vegetable soup that changes with the seasons. This version suits autumn and winter.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery sticks, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 can (14 oz) chopped tomatoes
  • 6 cups (1.4 L) vegetable stock
  • 1 can cannellini beans, drained
  • 2 cups chopped cabbage or kale
  • 1/2 cup small pasta shapes

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the oil for 10 minutes. Add garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Add tomatoes and stock, bring to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes.
  3. Add beans, greens and pasta; simmer until the pasta is tender.
  4. Season to taste and serve with olive oil and grated hard cheese, if you like.

Tip: if freezing, leave out the pasta and add it fresh when reheating.

Pesto pasta with broccoli

  • Serves 4
  • 25 min
  • Vegetarian

Cooking the broccoli in the same pot as the pasta saves time and washing up.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) short pasta
  • 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
  • 2 cups basil leaves
  • 1/3 cup toasted pine nuts or walnuts
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 cup grated hard cheese
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • Juice of half a lemon

Method

  1. Blend basil, nuts, garlic, cheese and oil to a rough paste; season with salt and lemon.
  2. Cook pasta in salted water; add broccoli for the final 3 minutes.
  3. Reserve a cup of cooking water, then drain.
  4. Toss with pesto, loosening with a splash of the reserved water.

Tip: cover leftover pesto with a thin layer of oil to keep it green.

Soft polenta with roasted vegetables

  • Serves 4
  • 45 min
  • Gluten-free

Creamy polenta is a wonderful, comforting base for any roasted or stewed vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (160 g) coarse polenta
  • 4 cups (950 ml) water or stock
  • 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 1/2 cup grated hard cheese (optional)
  • 4 cups mixed vegetables (peppers, courgettes, onions)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil and dried oregano

Method

  1. Roast the vegetables with oil, salt and oregano at 425°F (220°C) for 30 minutes.
  2. Bring the water to a boil, whisk in the polenta slowly and lower the heat.
  3. Cook, stirring often, until thick and creamy (time varies by brand).
  4. Stir in butter and cheese, then spoon onto plates and top with the vegetables.

Tip: check the packet — quick-cook polenta is ready in about 5 minutes.

Easy tray focaccia

  • 1 tray
  • Rise 2 hr
  • Bake 25 min

A no-fuss, no-knead focaccia that turns a bowl of soup or salad into a proper dinner.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (500 g) bread flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 2 cups (475 ml) lukewarm water
  • 1/3 cup olive oil, divided
  • Toppings: cherry tomatoes, olives, rosemary, flaky salt

Method

  1. Mix flour, salt, yeast and water into a sticky dough. Cover and rest 1 hour.
  2. Pour half the oil into a baking tray, add the dough and stretch gently to the corners. Rest 1 hour more.
  3. Dimple all over with oiled fingers, add toppings and drizzle with the rest of the oil.
  4. Bake at 450°F (230°C) for 20–25 minutes until deep golden.

Tip: focaccia is best the day it is made; toast leftovers for bruschetta.

Tomato, basil & white bean salad

  • Serves 2–4
  • 15 min
  • No cooking

A summery plate that becomes dinner when you add beans and good bread.

Ingredients

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 can cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • A handful of basil leaves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar or lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Soak the onion slices in cold water for 10 minutes to soften their bite; drain.
  2. Arrange tomatoes on a platter and season with salt.
  3. Scatter over beans, onion and basil.
  4. Whisk oil and vinegar, spoon over and serve with toasted bread.

Tip: use the ripest tomatoes you can find and never refrigerate them.
